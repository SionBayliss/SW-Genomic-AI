About the group

We are a of researchers and data scientists working at the intersection of genomics and artificial intelligence. We are broadly interested in AI methods applied to genomics data and it's derivatives. We have a diverse membership based at the universities of Bath, Bristol, Cardiff and Exeter, and we meet regularly to share methods, data and ideas.

What we aim to do

  • Connect people. Bring together genomics and machine learning researchers across the South West.
  • Share tools and data. Identify effective pipelines, models and consider approaches to establish good practice.
  • Train the next generation. Support workshops and seminars for PhD students and early-career researchers.
  • Win joint funding. Build collaborative bids with regional strength in genomic AI.
