Who we are

We are a network of researchers, clinicians and data scientists working where genomics meets artificial intelligence. Members are based at the universities of Bath, Bristol, Cardiff and Exeter and partner NHS trusts, and we meet regularly to share methods, data and ideas.

What we aim to do

  • Connect people. Bring together genomics and machine learning researchers across the four institutions.
  • Share tools and data. Pool pipelines, models and good practice so groups don’t solve the same problem twice.
  • Train the next generation. Run workshops and seminars for PhD students and early-career researchers.
  • Win joint funding. Build collaborative bids with regional strength in genomic AI.
